Privacy Policy

Effective: November 8, 2026 · Applies to the game "Dice Clicker Dungeon" (iOS / Android / Steam)

In short

The game has no account registration and never asks for your email address or a password. No gameplay data is sent to our servers.

Your save data is stored only on your device.

The mobile versions may use an advertising service (Google AdMob or AppLovin). For ads, information such as your advertising ID may be sent to these services.

Purchases are handled by the stores (App Store, Google Play, Steam). We never receive your payment details.
